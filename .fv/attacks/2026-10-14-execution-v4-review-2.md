### 1. One member's observation failure makes a shared success ineligible for unaffected members [severity: serious]
- **Category**: contradiction
- **Affected**: `FV/docs/execution-evidence-v4.md:414,418,456,480-484`; Dossier EX-004 associations at `D/.fv/execution-policy.json:6398-6403,6442-6447,6512-6517`.
- **What's wrong**: EX-004 is shared by C-01, C-02 and C-04. C-01 and C-02 have no additional required witness observations for this slot; C-04 requires `wPendingByteCapExact` and `wPendingByteCapRejected` (`D/.fv/integration-obligations.json:1933-1954`). Consider a real physical PASS at history sequence 10, a valid C-01 observation PASS at 11, a valid C-02 PASS at 12, and a genuine C-04 observation FAIL at 13 because a required witness is absent. The contract says this shared output may pass one member and fail another. However, candidate eligibility now requires the execution sequence to exceed every cutoff “for all its current members.” EX-004's descriptor includes all three members, so sequence 10 becomes ineligible even for C-01 and C-02, which have no failing observation or relevant cutoff. Claim PASS requires an eligible invocation, and `records:["C-01"]` cannot remove C-04 from the descriptor. The producer must either reject/re-execute valid C-01 evidence or disregard the all-members rule. Keeping the overall policy FAIL because C-04 failed is correct; invalidating C-01's independently satisfied association is not. This regression also defeats the intended per-execution reuse without improving C-04's failure visibility.
- **Cite (spec)**: > “A shared output may pass one member's positive witness and fail another's; retain both results.” (`FV/docs/execution-evidence-v4.md:418`)
  > “Candidate eligibility requires its own actual execution PASS event sequence strictly greater than every such cutoff for all its current members, plus subsequent PASS observations referencing that same actual attempt.” (`:480`)
  > “PASS requires all current-descriptor slots/observations to PASS with eligible later actual invocations and no blocking cutoff, plus canonical dependency/tool coverage.” (`:414`)
- **Cite (intent)**: > “each member still requires its own producer-bound claim record and complete member-specific observation checks.” (`D/docs/intent.md:32`, I12)
  > “For each canonical claim/tool identity across descriptor keys and policy/slot/recipe renamings, a later genuine execution or claim-observation failure, or indeterminate completion, blocks any earlier success...” (`D/docs/intent.md:32`, I12)
- **Fix recommendation**: Separate physical checkpoint validity from eligibility for a particular canonical claim/tool association. A genuine execution failure applies to every recorded member of that execution. An observation failure applies to its selected member. Evaluate reuse for each member against that member's applicable cutoff while preserving the complete descriptor and history. In the trace above, C-01 and C-02 may retain/revalidate their references to sequence 10; C-04 remains FAIL and cannot clear its barrier through observation-only rechecking. The full policy verdict must remain FAIL. Add this exact mixed-member trace to the acceptance checklist.
